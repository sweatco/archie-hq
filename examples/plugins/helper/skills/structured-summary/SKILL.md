# Structured Summary

Lead with a one-sentence conclusion. Use a paragraph or brief bullets for distinct requested facts, without repeating the conclusion. Keep every requested fact and decision-changing caveat; put uncertainty where it matters. Plain language; expand acronyms on first use. Short input normally needs one sentence. Never invent source facts.

The PM loads this skill directly; `helper:assistant` preloads it for workers.
